# Become a DMX Toolkit tester

DMX Toolkit is a free Android app for lighting technicians: test fixtures, check a patch, scan and address RDM devices, fixture library with GDTF import. It works over Wi-Fi with Art-Net and sACN nodes.

Before the app can go public, Google needs at least 12 testers for 14 days. Thank you for helping!

## Steps

1. **Join the testers group** (with the Google account you use on your phone):
   [groups.google.com/g/dmxtoolkit](https://groups.google.com/g/dmxtoolkit)
2. **Opt in to the test** on your phone:
   [play.google.com/apps/testing/com.dmxfun.toolkit](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.dmxfun.toolkit) → "Become a tester".
3. **Install the app** from Google Play:
   [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dmxfun.toolkit](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dmxfun.toolkit)
4. **Open it at least once and keep it installed for 14 days.**

You need an Android phone and a Google account. No lighting gear is required; if you do have an Art-Net or sACN node, any feedback is very welcome.

Feedback and questions: dmxtoolkit@gmail.com

The app is free, has no ads and no tracking. [Privacy policy](../toolkit/).

## Как стать тестировщиком

1. **Вступите в группу тестировщиков** с тем Google-аккаунтом, что на телефоне: [groups.google.com/g/dmxtoolkit](https://groups.google.com/g/dmxtoolkit)
2. **Подтвердите участие** с телефона: [play.google.com/apps/testing/com.dmxfun.toolkit](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.dmxfun.toolkit) → «Стать тестировщиком».
3. **Установите приложение** из Google Play: [ссылка на страницу приложения](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dmxfun.toolkit)
4. **Откройте его хотя бы раз и не удаляйте 14 дней.**

Нужны Android-телефон и Google-аккаунт. Пульт и приборы не обязательны, но если есть узел Art-Net или sACN — буду рад отзыву.

Вопросы и отзывы: dmxtoolkit@gmail.com
